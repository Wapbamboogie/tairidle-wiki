# Ability Field Effects

Many abilities in this game do more than they do in the mainline titles. On top of their normal battle behavior, a lot of them carry a **field effect** — a passive bonus that applies while the Pokémon is in your party, affecting things like item drops, encounter rates, prize money, breeding speed, and how long your incenses last.

This page lists every ability with a known field effect, grouped by what it actually influences. Unless noted otherwise, the ability holder just needs to be in your party for the effect to apply.

The benefits of these abilities are increased to 1.125x, 1.25x or 1.5x its initial value if the Pokemon is a Shiny, Chroma or Prismatic Variant 
---

## Battle Rewards

Effects that trigger on winning battles — extra items, extra money, extra drops.

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Gluttony** | Collected berries have a **25% chance** to render into **10 Warding Dust**. |
| **Ball Fetch** | About **1 in 500** won battles fetches a ball — and **1 in 100** of those fetched is a **Master Ball**. |
| **Hustle** | Trainer prize money is **×2**. Stacks with the Amulet Coin. |
| **Power of Alchemy** | About **1 in 100,000** won battles transmutes a random Incense. |
| **Harvest** | **1 in 300** chance to forage **2–10 berries** after a battle. |
| **Pickup** | **1 in 300** chance to acquire one item from a specific pool per battle. |
| **Honey Gather** | **1 in 50** chance to acquire **2–10 Honey** per battle. |
| **Super Luck / Compound Eyes** | Increases the chance for a wild Pokémon to hold its item, and **doubles** the chance to drop a **Game Corner Coin**. |
| **Frisk** | *In Battle:* prioritizes Thief when a wild Pokémon holds an item, putting it directly into the bag. *As Support:* doubles the chance of the opponent holding a berry. |
| **Magician / Pickpocket** | *As Support:* doubles the chance of the opponent holding a berry. |

## Exploration & Encounters

Effects on what you find out in the world and which Pokémon show up.

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Illuminate** | Zone treasure is **×2**. |
| **Keen Eye** | The rarest Pokémon on your route appears **twice as often**. Applied *after* honey and incense. |
| **Magnet Pull** | Draws in wild Pokémon matching its held **type-boost item**. |
| **Stench** | **25% chance** to not use up a Repel. |
| **Intimidate / Unnerve** | Raises the **Repel cap by 5**. |

## Party & Wild Behavior

Effects on your own party's condition and on the traits of the Pokémon you meet.

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Healer** | **20% chance** after won wild battles to cure a party member's status. |
| **Synchronize** | **50% chance** for a wild Pokémon to copy the nature of the ability holder. |
| **Cute Charm** | **2/3** of wild Pokémon become the **opposite gender** of the ability holder. |

## Breeding & Day Care

Effects that speed up eggs and Day Care progress.

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Flame Body / Magma Armor / Steam Engine** | Doubles **egg hatching speed**. |
| **Early Bird** | Day Care runs **20% faster**. |

## Utility

Incense modifiers. These two will stack together meaning you can reap the benefit of Pressure while alleviating its Penalty with Aroma Veil or Sweet Veil.

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Aroma Veil / Sweet Veil** | Incenses work **25% longer**. |
| **Pressure** | Makes incense effects **1.5× stronger** , but **halves** the time they burn for. |

---

## Notes

- Abilities listed together on one row (e.g. *Flame Body / Magma Armor / Steam Engine*) share the same field effect — you only need one of them.
- **Keen Eye** applies after honey and incense, so it multiplies whatever rarity weighting those have already set up rather than replacing it.
